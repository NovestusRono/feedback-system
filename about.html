ABOUT NYOTA ELECTRONICS

A Nairobi electronics retailer that listens to its customers and acts on
what they tell us.

Who we are

Nyota Electronics is an electronics retail business based in Nairobi, Kenya. We sell products that people rely on every day, and we know that a good purchase does not end at the till. What customers tell us after they buy, whether it is praise, a problem or an idea, helps us serve the next customer better.

Why this feedback portal exists

Customer comments used to arrive in many places: paper slips, phone calls, WhatsApp messages and conversations in the shop. Things could be missed, and customers did not always hear back. This portal brings every complaint, compliment and suggestion into one place, so each one is recorded, given to the right person and followed through to the end.

What we stand for

We listen

Every piece of feedback is read, whether it is a complaint, a compliment or a suggestion.

We take responsibility

Each case is given to a named member of our team who works on it until it is resolved.

We recognise good service

Compliments reach the people who earned them, and suggestions help us improve how we work.

How your feedback is handled

1

You submit

Tell us what happened and rate our service from 1 to 5. You receive a reference number.

2

A manager assigns it

Our manager reviews the feedback and gives it to the right member of staff.

3

We work on it

The assigned staff member looks into it and keeps a record of the work.

4

It is resolved

We record what was done. If you signed in, you can see the outcome in My Feedback.

Ways to share your feedback

Complaint

Something went wrong with a product or with our service. Tell us so we can put it right.

Compliment

Someone did a great job, or a product impressed you. Let us pass the praise on.

Suggestion

You have an idea that would make shopping with us better.

You can sign in with a free customer account to follow your feedback and read our replies. If you prefer not to sign in, you can send feedback anonymously. Anonymous feedback is read by our team, but because we do not know who sent it, we cannot reply or let you track it.

Your privacy

Only authorised Nyota Electronics staff can see feedback, and each team member sees only the cases that concern their work. A signed-in customer can see only their own feedback. Anonymous feedback is shown to staff as "Anonymous User" with no personal details.

It only takes a few minutes, and every response is read.
